Help Center (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions or log in to manage your bookings.


What brands of cosmetics do you use?

We use only premium, hypoallergenic professional brands such as MAC, Charlotte Tilbury, Tom Ford, and Make Up For Ever to ensure long-lasting results and skin safety.

How should I prepare my skin before the appointment?

Please arrive with a clean face. Avoid using heavy night creams or oils the day before. Gentle exfoliation and a light moisturizer are highly recommended.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes, but please let us know at least 48 hours in advance. Late rescheduling might lead to the forfeiture of your booking deposit.
