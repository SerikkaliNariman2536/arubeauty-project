Book Your Appointment

We are thrilled that you have chosen Arubeauty for your makeup needs. Because we operate on a flexible schedule and work strictly by appointment, advanced booking is required to secure your desired date and time. Please fill out the form below with your accurate details. Once submitted, our team will reach out to confirm the appointment.

When booking for a wedding or a large event, we highly recommend scheduling a trial session at least one month in advance. This allows us to discuss your vision, test out different color palettes, and ensure that you are completely satisfied with the final result before your big day. Our studio is equipped with multiple stations, so if you are booking the 'Do It Yourself' pair course, you and your friend will have ample space to practice comfortably.

Before you book, please familiarize yourself with our studio policies regarding deposits, cancellations, and preparation. We want your experience to be seamless and stress-free.

Booking Deposit
A non-refundable deposit is required to lock in your date. The remaining balance is due on the day of the service.
Cancellation Policy
If you need to cancel, please notify us at least 48 hours in advance. Late cancellations will result in a forfeit of the deposit.
Preparation
Please arrive with a clean, makeup-free face. Avoid using heavy moisturizers right before your appointment.
