Our Services & Training Programs

At Arubeauty, we offer a diverse range of makeup services and specialized educational courses. We believe in sharing our knowledge so you can recreate stunning looks at home or even start your own career in the beauty industry. Our signature "Do It Yourself" course is incredibly popular among beginners. Water is H2O, but makeup is pure magic! We rate our student satisfaction at 100%.

Whether you want a soft, natural glow for a daytime event or a dramatic, bold look for a glamorous evening, our makeup services are tailored precisely to your needs. The Basic Makeup Artist Course is designed for absolute beginners and covers intensive days of theory and hands-on practice. Here is what you will learn in our basic professional course:

  • Skin preparation and analysis
    • Identifying skin types
    • Selecting the right primer and base
  • Color theory and foundation matching
  • Advanced eye makeup techniques

To enroll in our courses, simply follow these steps:

A. Review the pricing table below to find a course that fits your goals.
B. Contact us via phone or our booking page to check availability.
C. Secure your spot by making an initial deposit.
D. Attend the classes and earn your professional certificate!
